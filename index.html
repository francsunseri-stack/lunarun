Your cycle

A training app that expects her body to change through the month.

Most plans are written as if every week feels the same. LunaRun gives her a place to log what is happening and lets that shape the day.

LOG

A cycle log beside the plan

Flow, symptoms and how much they are getting in the way, in a few taps on Today. She can add a note for herself.

EASE

Hard day? The run eases.

When symptoms are getting in the way and a hard session is on the calendar, LunaRun offers to turn it into an easy run. She decides, every time.

YOU

Private by design

The cycle log is stored on her iPhone. It is not sent to our servers and her coach cannot see it.

HLTH

Bring her history

If she already tracks in Apple Health, LunaRun can read past flow with her permission so the log does not start empty.

OFF

Off until she turns it on

Cycle tracking is optional. Leave it off and LunaRun is a complete training app without it.

SEE

Her pattern over time

A summary of recent cycles, so she can see what is typical for her and plan the big days around it.

Common questions

Who can see my cycle log?

Only you. It is stored on your iPhone, is not sent to our servers and is not shared with a coach. See the privacy policy.

Do I have to track my cycle?

No. Cycle tracking is off until you turn it on, and everything else works without it.

Does LunaRun change my plan on its own?

No. When symptoms are getting in the way it offers to ease that day's hard run. Nothing changes unless you say yes.

Is LunaRun medical advice?

No. It is a training tool. Talk to your doctor about anything that worries you, including missed or irregular periods.

How is it different from OffTrail?

Same training engine, with the cycle log, a plan that eases on hard days, and coaches who are all women.

When does it launch?

It is in development now. Email us and we will tell you when it is on the App Store.
